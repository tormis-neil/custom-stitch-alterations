What she does

From a hem to a gown rebuilt from the seams out

Every job starts with a fitting in the studio. Quotes are given in person, once she has seen the garment on you.

Bridal

The work that takes the most skill and the most time.

  • Hems, including horsehair and lace
  • Bustles — American, French, ballroom
  • Taking in or letting out the bodice
  • Adding sleeves, straps, or a modesty panel
  • Bridesmaids and mother-of-the-bride

Special Occasion

Quinceañera, prom, homecoming, and evening wear made to fit the person wearing it.

  • Resizing bodices and waists
  • Strap and neckline adjustments
  • Hemming full and layered skirts
  • Beading and appliqué repair

Everyday & Repairs

The ordinary things, done properly and returned quickly.

  • Trouser and skirt hems
  • Taking in shirts and dresses
  • Zipper replacement
  • Buttons, seams, and tears

About

I have been sewing since I was a girl

I learned on my mother's machine, and I have been altering wedding gowns ever since. When you bring me a dress, I am the one who pins it, I am the one who sews it, and I am the one who hands it back to you.

There is no counter here and no queue of anonymous tailors. There is a studio on Old Bullard Road, a fitting mirror, and enough time set aside to get your gown right before the day you need it.

Come in with the shoes you plan to wear. We will start there.
